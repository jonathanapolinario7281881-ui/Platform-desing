/*COMMON*/

Thin top bar — the same bar in every window (fit test at 1536 px)

Every separate window (Review, Positions & Orders, Settings, Stock detail, AI Assistant, News & Catalysts, System Health) has this bar and the 22 px alert line under it. The main window keeps its full top bar. Numbers are examples.

RULES

AUTO in every window. The state is always in words: AUTO OFF — YOU TRADE · AUTO ON — ENGINE CAN TRADE · AUTO PAUSED — reason · AUTO BLOCKED — reason. While AUTO is on, STOP AUTO is one click here. Turning AUTO on and Resume AUTO: main window only (hold 2 s + checks); the paused block's hover says so.
Blink chips only for stocks that matter to you: My list, the Level 2 slots, the Selected stock and all open positions — yours and the engine's (teal underline = engine's position). EXIT? (your positions only) comes first. Newest 3 shown, then "+N more". A chip blinks about 5 s, stays solid while the signal is active, and goes when it ends. Click = pop-up trade card.
Fit rule. Nothing wraps; FLATTEN ALL can never be pushed off the edge. When space runs out: (1) the window name shortens, (2) chips shorten to arrow + ticker, (3) the "LIVE ALERTS" label hides, (4) chips drop into "+N more". Safety states never disappear to save space: PAPER/LIVE, AUTO BLOCKED/PAUSED, SOFT/HARD LIMIT when active, health and FLATTEN ALL always stay; the full reason goes in the alert line when the bar wording is short. The LIMIT tag shows only when a limit is reached. LIVE puts the red frame around every window. Chip count and timings are TEST values.
