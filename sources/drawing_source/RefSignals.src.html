Signals, colors, sounds and alerts — reference page (v4, replaces File 9 v3 §8)

From the settled decision log (sections 5, 5b, 5c, 5f, 5h, 7d, 7f, 7g). Times and thresholds are starting values (TEST), tuned in paper testing.

If it blinks, it comes from the engine. A badge that doesn't blink comes from the AI. Every color also has a word or symbol, so color is never the only clue. Problems never blink — they use steady banners and sound.

COLORS — ONE JOB EACH

Green — up, buy, profit
Red — down, sell, loss, danger
Purple — short
Pink — EXIT? only
Teal — the engine's positions (and would-have trades, dashed)
Blue — selection and navigation
Grey — halted, unavailable, disabled
Amber — "be careful", always with a word: AI CAUTION, SPR box, SIZE LIMITED, FULL CAP, soft limit, give-back, DEGRADED
Yellow — things you set: My list ★, PINNED, your STOP line
White box — big order showing in the Level 2 book (information, not support)

ALERT LEVELS

LEVELEVENTS
INFO
quiet line		Fills (short one-time sound for yours, a softer one for the engine's), rank jumps, Level 2 swaps.
ATTENTION
sound + amber/pink line		EXIT?, soft limit, give-back, partial fill, AI turns NO-GO on a stock you hold, BUY/SHORT rejected, IBKR needs login / Level 2 down (DEGRADED), news / SEC / FDA / AI source down.
CRITICAL
own sound every ~10 s until SEEN; red line		Exit rejected, EXIT NOT FILLED, FLATTEN INCOMPLETE, hard limit, NOT SAFE TO TRADE (Alpaca, Massive, clock), halt on a stock you hold, intraday margin call, broker sync mismatch, AUTO BLOCKED — ORDER LIMIT (order circuit breaker tripped).
SEEN stops the repeating sound only — the line stays red until the problem is really resolved. Priority Critical > Attention > Info; a lower alert never covers an unresolved critical one ("+1 more"). In LIVE, critical sounds can be turned down but not off. The alert line never moves anything on screen; clicking it opens today's alert history (time, ticker, event, seen / resolved).

SOUNDS

A different sound per event, each on/off in Settings:
green ▲ · red ▼ (only for stocks that matter to you: My list, the Level 2 slots, the Selected stock, open positions — other blinks are visual) · EXIT? · your fill · the engine's fill (softer) · position closed · order rejected · EXIT NOT FILLED · stop triggered · halt / resume on a held stock · soft / hard limit · FLATTEN COMPLETE / INCOMPLETE · connection problem / AUTO blocked.

Anti-spam: the same stock + signal sounds at most about once a minute; critical sounds always play. Sounds and blinks are drawn from events the engine already produced — they never delay it.

HEALTH WORDS

HEALTHY — every check passes · DEGRADED — something is missing, trading still safe · NOT SAFE TO TRADE — new entries blocked; exits available whenever Alpaca can be reached. Word + dot in every top bar and on the phone.

LATER
