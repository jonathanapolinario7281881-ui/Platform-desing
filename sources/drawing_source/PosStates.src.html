Positions & Orders — states and examples

Companion to the two window drawings. Every state is a word or label, never color alone. Numbers are placeholders.

1 · ORDER STATUS — every order shows exactly one of these (Alpaca is the source)

RISK CHECK…
Checked by the platform before sending (size, cash, limits, session, duplicates).
SENT · waiting for Alpaca
Has a unique order ID, so a retry can never create a second order.
WORKING · 583 @ ≤ $2.24
Entry being worked up to its ceiling (2c). CANCEL on your own orders.
PARTIAL · 300/521
Filled shares are already a position; the rest keeps working.
FILLED · 583 @ $2.19
One-time fill sound (5f). Moves to Fills.
CANCEL PENDING CANCELLED · by you
Also “by STOP AUTO”, “by CANCEL ALL ENTRIES”, “by FLATTEN ALL”. A late fill after a cancel is still shown and counted.
NOT FILLED · price ran above $2.24
Stopped at the ceiling. Filled shares are kept (PARTIAL).
REJECTED · not shortable EXIT REJECTED
Rejected entry = Attention (no new risk). Rejected exit = Critical (you're exposed) (5f).
UNKNOWN · sent 1.8 s ago, no answer · checking with Alpaca
A timeout or dropped connection after sending. The platform asks Alpaca what happened and never resends until Alpaca confirms. New entries in that stock wait until it's resolved; exits of other positions are not affected.
EXPIRED · end of session
An order Alpaca ends at the session close.
YOUR STOP · triggers at $13.40
Your optional stop is a working item with move / ×. Where it's held (Alpaca or the platform) is still OPEN.

4 · BROKER CHECK (ALPACA) — in sync, checking, and two levels of mismatch

IN SYNC0 differences · 07:42:30
Positions, orders, cash and buying power all match.
CHECKINGafter reconnect · 3 s
After a reconnect or restart: rebuilt from Alpaca, never assumed empty. New AUTO entries wait for the result.
STOCK MISMATCH · ABCD
sharesplatform 300 · Alpaca 521
Alpaca's number used. No new entries in ABCD only; everything else keeps trading; exits work. Incident in Review.
ACCOUNT MISMATCH · AUTO BLOCKED — BROKER SYNC
cashplatform $522.80 · Alpaca $498.10
Critical. Cash, buying power or account totals differ, so no size can be trusted: all new entries blocked (yours and AUTO); exits always work.
