Companion to the System Health drawing. Every state is a word, never color alone. The band always says what still works. Times and amounts are examples; limits marked TEST are set by testing.

3 · CONNECTION ROW STATES

connected0.3 s ago
Normal. Last message time always shown — never an "OK" without a time.
SLOW · 4.2 s delay
Delay above the limit (TEST). Health unchanged until it becomes stale.
RECONNECTING… attempt 2 · 3 s
Automatic retries; each attempt logged. Data gaps are repaired after.
NEEDS LOGIN since 07:44:10
IBKR asks for a fresh login about once a week. Only Level 2 is affected.
STALE since 07:44:02
Connected but no fresh data — treated like down for new entries.
DOWN since 07:41:05
No connection. The band and the alert line say what it blocks.
Settings · Level 2 strategiesON · UNAVAILABLE — IBKR L2 DOWN
A failure never changes your settings (S6): the setting stays ON and shows it can't run; it runs again when the connection is back.

5 · WHEN A PROBLEM CLEARS — AUTO CONTINUES ONLY AFTER VERIFIED RECOVERY

07:44:10Massive stale → NOT SAFE TO TRADE07:44:13data back → RECOVERING · checks run07:44:15all checks pass → HEALTHY · AUTO CONTINUES
Checks: connection/data healthy · positions and orders match Alpaca · no UNKNOWN order · no unresolved gap affecting an active strategy, open position or pending order · clock and risk valid.
07:50:00Alpaca connection lost → NOT SAFE TO TRADE07:52:05back → RECOVERING · 1 UNKNOWN order (ABCD sell 100)07:52:08AUTO STAYS BLOCKED — press Resume AUTO · reason: order status uncertain
Anything uncertain → you resume (hold 2 s). Repeated drops (e.g. 3 in 15 minutes, TEST) also need your Resume AUTO.
Never resumes by itself, however short: intraday margin call · hard daily limit · FLATTEN ALL · your STOP AUTO · an unresolved broker mismatch · the soft-limit and give-back pauses (settled). Your positions are never sold because of a disconnect. What the engine does with its own positions during a long outage is decided in reliability testing (OPEN).
