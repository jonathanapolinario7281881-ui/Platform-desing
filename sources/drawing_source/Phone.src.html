Phone view — watch and stop from anywhere

For when the laptop is closed: the engine runs on the cloud server, so trading continues without the laptop. The phone shows the state and can stop it; it never buys. Numbers are examples (same day as the other drawings); timings are TEST values.

The phone can: see PAPER / LIVE, AUTO, health, Day P&L, equity, the loss meter, alerts and every open position · STOP AUTO (hold 1 s) · FLATTEN ALL (hold 1 s) · SELL/EXIT your own positions (hold 1 s, the same protected exit as the desk; SELL LOWER TO $x also needs the hold). Every phone action is logged "from phone".
The phone never: buys or shorts · turns AUTO on or resumes it · switches PAPER ↔ LIVE (display only) · changes settings · OVERRIDE EXIT or CANCEL ENGINE ENTRY (engine positions only through FLATTEN ALL). Exits lock, with the reason, when the phone's data is old or offline, when execution isn't healthy, or when that stock has an UNKNOWN order.
