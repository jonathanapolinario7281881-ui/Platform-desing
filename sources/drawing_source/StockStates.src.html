2 · PIN TO LEVEL 2 — FOUR STATES

📌 PIN TO LEVEL 2
AUTO OFF · puts this stock into a slot and keeps it there. The engine gives up its weakest slot that isn't showing one of your positions.
📌 PINNED · L2·2 · unpin
One pin at a time. Stays until you unpin or AUTO turns on (same rule as the panel pin).
PIN UNAVAILABLE · all slots hold your positions
Never forces out a slot showing a stock you hold.
PIN RELEASED · AUTO ON
The engine needs all three slots while AUTO is on.

3 · NO LEVEL 2 SLOT — THE TRADING LINE SAYS WHAT IS TRUE

NO LEVEL 2 SLOT · TRADING AVAILABLE
Only when Massive and Alpaca are healthy, the stock is eligible and not halted. Quotes and Time & Sales come from Massive.
NO LEVEL 2 SLOT · NOT SAFE TO TRADE — ALPACA EXECUTION UNAVAILABLE
The real restriction is shown instead; buttons follow the settled connection rules.
NO LEVEL 2 SLOT · HALTED · 2:40 elapsed
Halt shown as elapsed time; an official resume time only when the exchange publishes one.

4 · OFFERING / DILUTION LINE — ALWAYS WITH SOURCE AND TIME

"None found" = nothing in the SEC search, not a guarantee. 90 days is a TEST value.

Shown with the filing time; the NEWS grade and the AI reading update from it.

A stale or missing source is said plainly, never shown as "none".
