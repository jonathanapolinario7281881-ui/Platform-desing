Who owns a position — reference page (v4, replaces File 9 v3 §7)

One of the most important rules of the platform, from Jonathan's answers and the settled v4 decisions (sections 3, 4, 5g, 5h, 7a, 7b of the decision log).

Whoever opened a position owns it. Switching AUTO on or off never hands a position from one owner to the other.

SAFETY RULES — NEW RISK VS YOUR POSITIONS

Hard controls may block new risk, but never close your positions on their own. A position of yours closes only by your exit, your stop, FLATTEN ALL, or a forced liquidation by the broker (shown as "CLOSED BY ALPACA · MARGIN" in the strip, Positions & Orders, the alert history and Review).

Soft loss limit / give-back — AUTO pauses; your manual trading stays allowed; Resume AUTO in the main window.
Hard loss limit — all new entries blocked, yours too (v3's open question, now settled); exits always allowed; no resume that day.
Intraday margin call — AUTO and your BUY/SHORT blocked; exits available; the engine sells only what it needs of its own; for yours a cure button "EST. SELL x TO CURE MARGIN" that runs only on your click.
Disconnects — never sell your positions; the engine's positions follow the fail-safe decided in reliability testing (OPEN).

The engine still knows about your positions: they use buying power and count toward account limits, so it sizes its own trades with them. It never trades them.
